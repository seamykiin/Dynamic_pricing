# Tensor Margin — Dynamic Pricing Engine & B2B Storefront

Autonomous reinforcement learning (PPO) & predictive machine learning demand forecasting platform for dynamic B2B pricing optimization.

## Features
- **Predictive ML Demand Forecaster**: Point elasticity calculation, revenue & profit curve simulations.
- **Enterprise Multi-Strategy Policies**: Profit Maximizer, Buy Box Undercut, Clearance Markdown, Brand Premium, and Margin Preservation.
- **Autonomous Volatility Dampening & Gross Margin Guardrails**: Guardrails protecting minimum acceptable profit margins.
- **Amazon B2B Storefront**: Live purchasing interface reflecting dynamic tiered quantity discounts.
- **Telemetry & Live Audit Logs**: Complete history of automated pricing decisions and overrides.
